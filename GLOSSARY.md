# MyCoffeeApp

A coffee shop app where customers browse coffee products, save the ones they like, and order drinks
for delivery.

## Language

**Product**:
A coffee drink offered in the menu, such as Latte or Espresso.
_Avoid_: Item, coffee (as a noun for the menu entry)

**Cart**:
The Products the customer has chosen to buy, each with a quantity. Titled "Order" on its screen.
_Avoid_: Order, basket

**Wishlist**:
The customer's saved set of Products they like, independent of size and quantity. Labelled "
Favorites" in the bottom navigation.
_Avoid_: Favorites, favourite list
